## Editorial decisions

Numerical outcomes are framed as experiments, calculations or simulation results. Applied CFD PDF figures are absent; the supplied TALL-3D image is contextual and is labelled accordingly. MIST is separate from coursework Labs C and D. Mars team CAD and third-party concept imagery are explicitly distinguished. The BSc Weibull calculation is an assumed-parameter educational model. No publication acceptance or unsupported individual task ownership is claimed. Sloshing verification at FDR stage is not claimed as completed physical testing.
