01 / THE QUESTION

Project objectives

The team developed a conceptual two-seat general-aviation electric aircraft, using the Pipistrel Velis Electro as a starting reference. The study connected weight estimates, wing and tail geometry, aerodynamic analysis, performance and trim.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Methods and contribution

  • Co-authored the ten-person Fundamentals of Flight design report.
  • The team used constraint diagrams and mass estimates to establish the preliminary configuration.
  • The documented workflow includes airfoil selection, aerodynamic and drag analysis, mission performance, load factors and longitudinal stability.
  • A complete aircraft configuration was analysed with VSPAero.

03 / THE OUTCOME

Results

The report’s conceptual design uses a 600 kg maximum take-off mass, a 12,000 ft cruise altitude and an 85 KCAS cruise condition. The estimated 90-minute endurance and calculated trim behaviour support the coursework concept under its stated assumptions.

04 / SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

These are conceptual calculations, not measured flight performance or certification evidence. The report itself notes that the VSPAero results should not be used to determine post-stall behaviour. Individual task ownership is not specified.
