What the project explored

MIST, the MIniature Student Satellite, provided experience with thermal analysis in a multidisciplinary spacecraft project. This work is separate from the thermal-vacuum and thermal-control coursework reports shown elsewhere in this portfolio.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

The work contributed to the team’s assessment of the satellite’s thermal behaviour and connected simulation choices with spacecraft subsystem requirements.
