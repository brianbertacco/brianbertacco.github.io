01 / THE QUESTION

What the project explored

Project Buzz Lightyear combined a deployable-wing water rocket with a partially filled sloshing tank. My work focused on the fluid dynamics of the tank and the comparison of annular and mixed baffle configurations.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

From problem to method

  • Led the baffled-tank CFD analysis for the two candidate designs, as documented in the team deliverable matrix.
  • Compared free-surface motion, lateral reaction force, centre-of-gravity displacement and oscillation frequency against the unbaffled baseline.
  • Contributed blockage-compliance calculations and risk assessment to the final design report.

03 / THE OUTCOME

What the work showed

The report predicts a reduction in peak lateral force from 15.4 N to 4.2 N for the mixed baffle, a 72.7% decrease relative to the unbaffled simulation. The baseline CFD frequency of 3.15 Hz was close to the analytical estimate of 3.11 Hz.

04 / SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

These are simulation results for the report’s excitation and fill conditions. At the final-design-report stage, five requirements still awaited physical testing. Flight predictions and team hardware results should be distinguished from the sloshing CFD.
