01 / THE QUESTION

What the project explored

Mjolnir is the ÆSIR team’s hybrid paraffin–nitrous-oxide rocket project. My experience brought together propulsion analysis, test instrumentation and practical work around static-fire campaigns.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

The project developed my experience connecting engineering analysis with instrumented hardware and test data in a student rocketry team.
