01 / THE QUESTION

What the project explored

My bachelor’s thesis reviewed radioisotope thermoelectric generators for space missions and the radiation environment that spacecraft and crews must withstand. It connected thermoelectric conversion with mission-level power and protection considerations.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

From problem to method

  • Reviewed RTG operating principles, fuel options and the Seebeck effect.
  • Compared radioisotope power with solar generation for different mission environments.
  • Developed an illustrative Weibull reliability analysis in MATLAB.
  • Examined radiation dosimetry, shielding materials and engineering and operational countermeasures.

03 / THE OUTCOME

What the work showed

The thesis presents the advantages and limitations of radioisotope power for long-duration missions and illustrates how assumed lifetime parameters influence reliability estimates.

04 / SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

The reliability curves use assumed Weibull parameters and are an educational model, not a validated prediction of MMRTG flight reliability. The work is a literature-based thesis with analytical illustration, not reactor or RTG hardware development.
