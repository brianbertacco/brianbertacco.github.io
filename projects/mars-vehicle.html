01 / THE QUESTION

Project objectives

The Blue Team’s Vehicle Design subgroup studied the adaptation of a Starship-based vehicle concept for a six-person Mars mission. The work integrated habitat arrangements with power, thermal control, communications, mass budgets and off-nominal scenarios.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Methods and contribution

  • Shared responsibility for power and thermal work with Koohyar Saebi, as recorded in the report appendix.
  • Contributed calculations and CAD for solar panels and radiators.
  • Worked within a multidisciplinary vehicle design that balanced subsystem needs with the overall mass and accommodation constraints.

03 / THE OUTCOME

Results

The team produced a vehicle concept with subsystem layouts, requirements, budgets and contingency considerations. My contribution connected power generation and thermal management to their physical integration in the vehicle.

04 / SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

This is an academic conceptual design, not a SpaceX project or a validated human-rated vehicle. Overall vehicle modelling and rendering are credited to other team members. Mission dates in the report are scenario assumptions.
